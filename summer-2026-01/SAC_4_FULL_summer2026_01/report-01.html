FULL camera.

Switched from LIDAR to FULL camera on the same Summer 2026-01 map. LIDAR only sees 19 rays until the dark track border, so the jumbotron wall was invisible scenery; the reward line was paying for a cut through that corner. FULL trains on window screenshots (resized to 64x64), so the car can see scenery, not just the asphalt edge. Look-ahead (CHECK_FORWARD) is 100 instead of 500. Fresh replay — not a resume of the LIDAR trains.
