Teaching an AI Super Mario Bros

A neural net learned the NES cart from pixels and a homemade score — not from a walkthrough. After 100 million environment steps it has beaten World 1 in order and opened World 2.

If you only read one paragraph. Sixteen Marios train at once by gambling. The window you watch on stream is the same network played greedy — always the single most likely button combo, never a dice roll. That is why the TV can look stuck on 1-1 while the learner has already reached 2-1. The number that looks like a score (train return) also lied for a long time: it was high when Mario farmed a suicide pit, and it dropped when he actually learned to jump. Campaign progress is sequential stages, not that number.

What it actually sees and does

Eyes

Not “there is a pit at x=670.” The emulator feeds the raw NES picture. We shrink it to 84×84 grayscale and stack four frames so motion exists. Frame skip 4 hides a lot of velocity, so we also paint how fast Mario is moving as the top row of that tiny image.

Hands

Twelve discrete actions: idle, run, jump, and the usual NES diagonals.

Score signal

Mostly: new horizontal progress on the current stage, plus a sticky bonus when a flag is first grabbed. A pile of anti-farm rules stop him getting paid for walking into a pit, sitting until TIME 000, or taking the 1-2 warp to World 3 and calling it done.

Brain

A Nature CNN: three small convolution layers, a 512-unit layer, then a policy head (12 buttons) and a critic head. Same family as the Space Invaders notebook on this site. PPO, not Q-learning.

Greedy vs the sixteen learners

The network outputs twelve probabilities. Greedy means take the largest one, every time — no dice. That is the NES window on stream. The sixteen trainers sample from the same list, so they still try the slightly-less-likely jump. Watch reloads the latest checkpoint between lives, so the TV is the current policy.

The plot of the first 100 million

  1. Walk right. 1-1 and 1-2 come for free enough. The 1-2 pipe to World 3 looks like a miracle on a bad overlay. It is a cheat. Sequential cart ignores it.
  2. Farm the first 1-3 pit. Die at the rim, respawn at the start, get paid the walk again. Train return soars into the 700s–1,000s. Greedy watch dies at the same ledge for tens of millions of steps. That is the spike on the chart, not World 8.
  3. Stop paying the cheat, show him speed. Falling in that pit earns nothing. Skip-4 had hidden velocity; we painted it. The jump appears. Return collapses. 1-4 opens. That collapse is the success.
  4. World 2. Sequential farthest is 2-1. Four stages flagged in order. About 13% of the full cart by pixel length.
